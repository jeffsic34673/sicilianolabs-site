Privacy Policy

Siciliano Labs

Siciliano Labs develops personal software applications for use by the owner and authorized users.

HomeDisplay

HomeDisplay is a personal home information display application running on a Raspberry Pi.

HomeDisplay may access Google Calendar information in order to display calendar events on the HomeDisplay device.

Google Calendar information is used solely for displaying calendar information within the HomeDisplay application. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

HomeDisplay requests read-only access to Google Calendar and does not modify or delete calendar events.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the owner of Siciliano Labs.
